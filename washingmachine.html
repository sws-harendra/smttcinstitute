What Students Will Learn

During this washing machine mechanic course Patna students learn the complete working system of washing machines. Component Testing & Diagnosis

In this washing machine mechanic course Patna, students get hands-on practice in testing motors, capacitors, inlet valves, drain pumps, pressure switches, timers, sensors and control boards. They also learn wiring diagrams and electrical connections to avoid unnecessary part replacement.

Installation & Servicing Procedures

Through this washing machine servicing training Patna, students learn correct installation methods for semi-automatic, top load and front load machines.
